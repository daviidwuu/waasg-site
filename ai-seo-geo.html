AI SEO & GEO

Rank on Google. Get cited by AI.

More and more customers ask ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google's AI Overviews before they click anything. GEO (generative engine optimization) makes your business easy for those systems to find, trust and cite. It builds on solid SEO, so I do both together.

What I do.

The work that helps both search engines and AI answer engines understand who you are, what you offer and where.

Technical SEO

Indexing, speed, mobile, broken links, redirects, titles and descriptions. Fixed directly where I have access, or handed over as a precise fix list.

Structured data

Organization, LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ, product and menu schema, so machines read your key facts correctly.

Pages that answer questions

Service, location, comparison and FAQ pages written for people first, in plain language that is easy to quote.

Google Business Profile and listings

Accurate hours, categories, services and photos, and the same name, address and phone everywhere it matters.

AI-answer tracking

A fixed set of real customer questions, checked every month in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews. You see who gets mentioned, who gets cited and how that changes.

Crawler access and llms.txt

Search and AI crawlers allowed in, a clean sitemap, and an llms.txt file that points AI tools at the pages that matter.

SEO and GEO, in one sentence each.

SEO gets your pages ranked in search results, so people click through to you.

GEO gets your business mentioned and cited inside AI-generated answers, often before anyone clicks.

They reward the same things: a fast site, clear facts, consistent listings, structured data and pages that answer real questions directly. That's why one plan covers both.

What I won't promise.

Nobody controls Google's rankings or what an AI model says. I won't guarantee positions or citations. Every month you'll see what I changed, what moved and what I'm doing next, so you can judge the work for yourself.

Questions about SEO and GEO.

How long does SEO take to work?

Technical fixes can show up within weeks, once search engines crawl your site again. Content and reputation usually take months. That's why every plan includes a plain-English monthly report, so you can see progress along the way.

Can you get my business into ChatGPT answers?

I can make your business easier for AI answer engines to find, understand and cite, and track whether it gets mentioned for the questions your customers ask. No one can guarantee what an AI model will say.

Do you need access to my website?

It helps. With access I make the fixes directly. Without it, you get a precise fix list your web person can follow.
