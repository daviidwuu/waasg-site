Pricing

Public prices. No surprises.

Three monthly plans, each including everything in the one before. Every plan starts with a free site snapshot, so you know what I'd work on before you pay anything.

Monthly plans

Visibility

Get found on Google and in AI answers.

US$600/month

Founding clients: US$420/month for the first 3 months.

Best for: one location or one main service.

  • Monthly technical SEO check (indexing, speed, broken links, titles, schema) with fixes made directly where I have access, or a clear fix list where I don't
  • AI-answer tracking: 20 questions your customers ask, checked every month in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews, showing who gets mentioned and cited
  • Google Business Profile kept accurate and active: hours, categories, services, photos and posts
  • Structured data (schema) and llms.txt set up and kept current
  • One new or improved page a month (service, location or FAQ) written for people, search engines and AI answers
  • Business name, address and phone kept consistent on the main directories
  • A plain-English monthly report: what I did, what changed, what's next
Start with Visibility

Growth

Turn visibility into enquiries.

US$1,800/month

Founding clients: US$1,260/month for the first 3 months.

Best for: multi-location businesses, or one location ready to push for more enquiries.

  • Everything in Visibility, for up to 5 locations or service areas
  • Four content pieces a month (articles, location pages, comparison pages) built around real customer questions
  • Conversion work: landing pages, forms, calls to action and booking or ordering links, tested and improved
  • Review requests sent to all recent customers, plus drafted replies to every new review
  • Email follow-up for new leads, set up in the email tool you already use
  • Tracking you can read: Search Console, GA4, and call, form and booking conversions
  • A monthly call plus the written report
Start with Growth

Automation partner

Take repetitive admin off your team.

US$3,500/month

Founding clients: US$2,450/month for the first 3 months.

Best for: multi-location operators and property managers with repetitive admin.

  • Everything in Growth
  • A new automation built every month and kept running: lead intake and routing, follow-ups, reporting, data cleanup or handoffs between your tools
  • AI-drafted replies and summaries for enquiries, reviews and admin, approved by your team before anything is sent
  • Works with the systems you already run, such as Buildium, AppFolio, POS or reservation tools, through their exports, built-in integrations or API where your plan allows
  • Small builds included: landing pages, internal dashboards, forms and simple tools
  • A weekly check-in, and replies within one business day
Start with Automation partner

Founding-client offer

The first 3 retainer clients get 30% off their first 3 months, in exchange for a short, honest testimonial once we've worked together. The discount doesn't depend on what the testimonial says, and any testimonial I publish will say it came from a founding client who got the discount.

Terms: 3-month minimum, then month to month with 30 days' notice.

Billing: Prices in US dollars, billed monthly in advance. If a paid tool is needed, you pay for it directly and only after you agree.

Projects: Websites, software, one-off automations and photography (Singapore only) are quoted per project, in writing, before any work starts.

Which plan is right?

Mostly need to be found?

One location or one main service, and customers can't find you on Google or in AI answers yet. Start with Visibility.

Found, but not enough enquiries?

Several locations, or traffic that isn't turning into calls, bookings and forms. Choose Growth.

Drowning in admin?

Your team spends hours on follow-ups, reports and copy-paste between tools. Choose Automation partner.
