How a fix gets built.

  1. Send the task.

    Describe it in a sentence or two: what happens today, who does it, and how often.

  2. Smallest fix, quoted.

    I map it, suggest the simplest system that removes it, and quote it in writing before anything is built.

  3. Built and handed over.

    Tested with your real data, documented in plain language, and shown to the people who'll use it.

Rules I build by.

  • AI drafts, people approve. Nothing goes to a customer without a human OK, unless you decide otherwise.
  • Your accounts, your data. Automations run in accounts you own wherever possible.
  • No surprise costs. If a tool has a running cost, you'll know before it's built.
  • Written down. You get a plain description of what runs where, and what to do if it stops.

One-off or ongoing.

Single automations are quoted per project. For a new automation every month plus upkeep, see Automation partner at US$3,500/month.

Which tools do you use?

Whatever fits what you already run. Often that's a no-code automation tool or a small script; sometimes it's an integration built into software you already pay for. I'll recommend the simplest option and tell you about any running costs before building.

Do you work with Buildium or AppFolio?

I can build around them: starting from their reports and exports, and using their API or built-in integrations where your plan allows. waa isn't affiliated with either company. See automation for property managers.
