What I check

  • Google: whether your pages are indexed, and what shows up for your name and your main service.
  • AI answers: what ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews say when asked about you and your category, and who they cite instead.
  • Speed and mobile: Lighthouse scores and the issues that slow your site down on phones.
  • Structured data and listings: whether search engines and AI can read your key facts, such as services, hours and location.
  • Getting in touch: how quickly a visitor understands what you do and how to contact you.

What you get

An email within 3 business days with what I found and the three fixes I'd make first, in priority order. Do them yourself, hand them to your web person, or ask me to do them.

What it costs

Nothing. No call, no obligation, no mailing list. If you'd like help afterwards, the prices are public.

Founding-client offer: the first 3 retainer clients get 30% off their first 3 months, in exchange for a short, honest testimonial once we've worked together. The discount doesn't depend on what the testimonial says, and any testimonial I publish will say it came from a founding client who got the discount. See pricing.

Snapshot questions.

Is the snapshot really free?

Yes. It's how new clients get to see how I work before paying anything. There's no obligation and you keep the results.

Do I have to get on a call?

No. Everything happens by email. If you'd like to talk it through afterwards, just reply.

What if I don't have a website yet?

The form needs a website address, so email your business name and location to david@waasg.com instead. The snapshot will look at your Google Business Profile, your listings and what AI answers say about businesses like yours.

What happens to my details?

They arrive as an email in one inbox, david@waasg.com, and are used only to prepare and send your snapshot. There's no mailing list and nothing is shared. The privacy page says how long they're kept and how to have them deleted.
