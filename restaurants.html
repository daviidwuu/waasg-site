For multi-location restaurants

Make every location easy to find and book.

When someone asks Google or ChatGPT where to eat nearby, each of your locations should show up with the right hours, menu and booking link. I help restaurant groups get that right, then automate the busywork around reviews and enquiries.

Get a free snapshot of your group See pricing

What usually goes wrong.

  • Location pages are thin or missing, so "near me" searches go to the place down the street.
  • Google Business Profiles have old hours, old photos or no menu.
  • Menus live in PDFs or images that search engines and AI tools read poorly.
  • Reviews go unanswered when nobody owns them across locations.
  • Catering and private-event enquiries sit in a shared inbox for days.

What I set up.

A real page per location

Address, hours, menu highlights, booking or ordering links and Restaurant schema, so each location can rank and be cited on its own.

Google Business Profiles

Every location kept accurate and active: hours, holiday hours, categories, menu links, photos and posts.

Menus machines can read

Menus as real web text with structured data, alongside the PDF if you like it.

Reviews, handled

Drafted replies for every new review, approved by your managers, and a simple way to ask all recent guests for one.

AI-answer tracking

Monthly checks of questions like "best ramen near Tanjong Pagar" or "where can 12 people have dinner tonight" in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews.

Enquiry automation

Catering, events and large-group requests routed to the right person fast, with a weekly digest of reviews and enquiries.

In Singapore, I can also photograph your dishes and dining rooms. About photography.

Which plan fits.

  • Visibility (US$600/month): a single location getting found.
  • Growth (US$1,800/month): a group with several locations that wants more bookings and enquiries.
  • Automation partner (US$3,500/month): larger groups with admin to take off managers' plates.
