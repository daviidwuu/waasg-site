Websites & software

Websites that load fast and get found.

Your website is your best salesperson, and the main thing AI answer engines read about you. I build fast, accessible sites with a clear offer and real text that search engines and AI can read, plus small tools when off-the-shelf software doesn't fit.

Get a free site snapshot Email about a project

Websites.

New builds and redesigns.

  • One page per service and location, so each can rank and be cited on its own.
  • Real text in the HTML, not hidden behind scripts that some crawlers never run.
  • Structured data, sitemap and crawler access set up from day one.
  • Fast on phones: light pages, no bloated page builder.
  • Accessible: readable contrast, labelled forms, works with a keyboard.
  • Lead capture wired in: forms, booking links, email or whatever you already use.

Small software.

When a spreadsheet stops being enough.

  • Internal dashboards that pull your numbers into one place.
  • Simple portals and forms for clients, owners or staff.
  • Integrations that move data between the tools you already pay for.
  • Small AI features: summaries, sorting and drafting, with a person reviewing the output.

You're looking at an example.

This site is plain HTML and CSS: no framework, no page builder, no tracking scripts. Every page has its own title, description and structured data, and the text is readable by any crawler, with or without JavaScript.

How projects are priced.

Websites and software are quoted per project, in writing, before any work starts. Ongoing care, updates and small builds can be part of a monthly plan. See pricing.

Which platform will you build on?

The one that suits how you'll run the site: plain HTML for speed, or a content management system your team can edit. I'll explain the trade-offs before you decide.

Can you improve our existing site instead?

Often, yes. The fastest win is frequently fixing the current site's structure, speed and copy rather than starting over. The free snapshot will tell you which.
