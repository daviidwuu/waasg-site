About

A tiny company that turns annoying work into working systems.

waa (Wu's Automation Agency) is a founder-led AI marketing, SEO, GEO and automation agency based in Singapore, run by David Wu. Clients can be anywhere; work outside Singapore is remote, and photography is offered in Singapore only.

How I work.

Proof first.

You see the problem and a working fix before you're asked to commit to more.

No bloated process.

No account managers or handoffs. You deal with the person doing the work.

Plain English.

Reports say what I did, what changed and what's next, without jargon.

AI drafts, people decide.

I use AI to move fast. Nothing goes out in your name without a human check.

Honest about being new.

No invented logos, testimonials or results. When there are real case studies, they'll be here.

You own your stuff.

Your website, content, accounts and data stay in your name.

What waa does.

  • AI SEO and GEO: getting found on Google and cited in AI answers.
  • AI marketing and growth: turning that visibility into enquiries.
  • Automation: removing repetitive work around leads, replies, reports and data.
  • Websites and software: fast sites and small tools.
  • Photography: for businesses in Singapore.

About the name.

waa is short for Wu's Automation Agency, and it's always written in lowercase. It started as a way to turn annoying work into small, useful systems. The work now covers the whole path from being found to following up, but the idea is the same: one bottleneck at a time.
