Privacy

Privacy, in plain English.

This site sets no cookies and runs no tracking scripts. The only personal data that reaches waa through it is what you choose to send by email, such as a snapshot request. Here's what that covers, why, how long it's kept and how to have it deleted, under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

What a snapshot request collects.

The snapshot form doesn't send or store anything by itself. It writes an email in your own email app, or in Gmail if you use that button, and nothing reaches me until you press send. The email holds what you typed:

  • your website address, your name and your business name;
  • your main goal and the plan you're curious about, if you chose them;
  • any notes you added;
  • your email address, and anything else you add before sending.

The same goes for any other email you send to david@waasg.com.

Why, and who sees it.

I use it only to prepare and send your snapshot and to reply to you. It isn't sold, shared or added to a mailing list.

It's stored in the david@waasg.com mailbox, which is hosted by Google Workspace, so it may be kept on servers outside Singapore.

How long it's kept.

Snapshot requests and the emails about them are deleted 12 months after our last exchange. If we start working together, I keep what the work needs while we work together, and invoices for as long as the law requires.

Ask me to delete your details sooner and I will.

No cookies or tracking.

The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and loads nothing from other companies' servers. If that changes, this page will say so first.

Like any website, it's delivered by a hosting provider, Vercel, which processes technical data such as IP addresses to serve the pages and keep the site secure.

See, correct or delete your data.

Email david@waasg.com to ask what I hold about you, to correct it, to have it deleted, or to withdraw your consent to its use. I'll reply within 30 days, and usually much sooner. The same address is waa's data protection contact.
