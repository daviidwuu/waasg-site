SEO and GEO

What is GEO?

GEO (generative engine optimization) means making your business easy for AI answer engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews to find, understand and cite. It builds on SEO: clear pages, structured data, consistent listings and content that answers real questions.

How is GEO different from SEO?

SEO gets your pages ranked in search results so people click through to you. GEO gets your business mentioned and cited inside AI-generated answers, often before anyone clicks. They reward the same foundations, so waa does both together.

Can you guarantee rankings or AI citations?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who does. Nobody controls Google's rankings or what an AI model says. What I can promise is clear work, tracked monthly, with a plain report of what changed.

How long until I see results?

Technical fixes can show up within weeks, once search engines crawl your site again. Content, reviews and reputation usually take a few months to build. The monthly report shows progress along the way.
